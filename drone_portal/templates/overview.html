Problem Overview

1. Background

In the year 2026, urban logistics have fully transitioned to autonomous drone fleets. City regulations have grown increasingly complex: No-Fly Zones are now dynamic areas (around scheduled stadium events, VIP convoys or construction sites) that activate and deactivate at specific times. The objective is a routing engine that manages a fleet of drones to deliver packages while navigating these shifting constraints and optimising limited battery life.

2. Problem Statement

  • Drones start at the warehouse, at the centre of the map: (map_size[0] / 2, map_size[1] / 2).
  • Each drone may carry multiple packages per trip, provided total weight does not exceed its max_payload.
  • After deliveries, a drone must return to the warehouse or a charging station; mid-air stops are prohibited.
  • All distances are Euclidean; drones fly straight lines between path points.
  • Drones do not collide; multiple drones may occupy the same coordinate.

3. Fixed Constants

QuantityValue
Drone speed1 distance unit per timestep
Battery capacity500 energy units (full at start)
Charge rate2 energy units per timestep

4. Energy Model

Energy consumed per leg is E_leg = distance × (1 + current_payload_weight). Payload decreases after each delivery, reducing the cost of later legs. Battery must always remain greater than or equal to zero.

5. No-Fly Zones

  • Circular or rectangular areas with a time window [T_start, T_end].
  • A drone may not enter or pass through an NFZ while it is active.
  • If a path is blocked, the drone waits at its current location (no battery is consumed while waiting).

6. Scoring

raw_score = (successful_deliveries × 100) − (total_energy × 0.1) − (makespan × 0.05)

A delivery counts only if the drone arrives at the exact delivery coordinates on or before the deadline, with no constraint violations. Invalid solutions score zero. Higher scores are better.
